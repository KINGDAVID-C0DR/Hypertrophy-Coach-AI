# Hypertrophy-Coach-AI
Free AI powered hypertrophy app that takes the guessing out of what exercises to do, how much weight to lift, and how many reps. Keeps track of progress and increases weight and reps as you get stronger. If you're just starting out and you dont know how much weigh to start with, the first time you perform a specific exercise, the app will take you thru a calculation phase where you choose a weight you can rep 20 times, then based on how close you get to 20 reps with that weight, it will calsulate your estimateed max for that exercise and the weight you should start out with. Give it a try! Inspired by the RP Hypertrophy App.
